A Marysville fixture since before State Ave filled in

Family owned and operated for over 30 years, Mai's Thai built its name on bold Thai and Vietnamese flavors and the kind of welcome that turns first-timers into regulars. You will find us in Prime Square, next to La Terraza — look for the hand-painted windows.
